02

Long-term Portfolio Plan

Built a moderate-growth, 80/20 stock-to-bond portfolio with monthly contributions, a no-sell rebalancing plan, and a 30-year projection.

FinanceExcelETFsPortfolio
+

Objective

Design a diversified long-term portfolio that grows steadily through regular contributions, with risk controlled by asset allocation and position limits.

Target allocation

Asset classTargetExp. return
US equity ETF (VTI)33%7.0%
Intl equity ETF (VXUS)12%6.5%
Dividend ETF (SCHD)5%6.5%
Individual stocks (NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, BRK.B)30%8.0%
Bonds (BND)15%4.0%
Cash / T-bills (SGOV)5%3.5%

Approach

  • Capped each individual stock at 6% of the portfolio so one broken thesis can't do major damage.
  • Built a holdings tracker that directs each month's contribution to the most underweight positions, so rebalancing needs no selling and no taxable sales.
  • Set a +/- 5 point drift tolerance and a quarterly status check to tilt future buys instead of panic-selling.
  • Projected 30 years of growth on $1,500/month, rising 3% a year, with low, base, and high return cases and inflation-adjusted values.

Results (base case)

  • Weighted expected return of about 6.6% a year.
  • About $284K after 10 years and $921K after 20 years.
  • About $2.26M after 30 years (roughly $1.08M in today's dollars) on about $856K contributed. The low and high cases are about $1.64M and $3.16M.

Illustrative template built on assumed returns, not guarantees or investment advice.
